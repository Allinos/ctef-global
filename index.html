About CTEF Global

Council for Teacher Education Foundation (CTEF), Regd. No. Nagpur/0000403/2019, earlier known as the Council for Teacher Education (CTE), was established in 1985 in Bhopal. The seed sown by Founder President Dr. Gulab Chaurasia with a handful of stalwarts in national education has grown into a large banyan tree with branches in 23 national and three overseas chapters.

Our prime objective is to develop competent, resourceful and skilful teaching professionals at every level of education, from lower primary to higher education, and in teacher education in particular. CTEF attained international status with the establishment of overseas chapters.

With this objective, CTEF chapters run teacher development programmes (TDP) throughout the year: workshops on recent pedagogy, classroom transactions, joyful and effective learning, explorative, interactive and project-based learning; seminars, conferences and symposiums; interactive meets with students and parents; reading materials, journals, newsletters and handbooks for teachers; student guidance and counselling, parental counselling, teacher training and teacher exchange programmes (TEP).

The Council is a professional home for teachers, teacher educators, educational administrators, policy makers, research scholars and professionals in education.

Leadership: Dr. K.M. Bhandarkar of Nagpur and Prof. Nilima Bhagabati of Guwahati, Assam have led the organisation since 9 November 2009 as National President and International Secretary General respectively.

Our vision

To create a strong and connected community of skilled teachers and teacher educators committed to quality education and continuous professional growth.

Our mission

  • To build a strong national and international network of teachers and teacher educators.
  • To develop teachers as competent and professional teaching practitioners.
  • To promote skilled teachers and quality teacher education through professional development and capacity-building initiatives.
